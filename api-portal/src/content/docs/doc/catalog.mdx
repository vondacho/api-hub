The Catalog is the discovery surface: every contract that has been onboarded and
registered is listed here, searchable, with its scorecard and its resolved
contract attached.

## What a catalog entry carries

- the **contract** itself, bundled and resolved — no unresolved `$ref`s to chase;
- the **scorecard**, per [dimension](/doc/design-guidelines/), from the last scoring run;
- **revision history** — a contract is versioned, and superseded revisions stay readable.
