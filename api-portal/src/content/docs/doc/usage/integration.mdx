How to go from a catalogued contract to a working client.

## Generate, do not hand-write

The contract is the source of truth. Generate your client from the resolved
contract published in the [Catalog](/doc/catalog/) rather than transcribing
types by hand — a hand-written client silently drifts, a generated one fails the
build when the contract moves.
